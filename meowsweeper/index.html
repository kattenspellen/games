How to play

Spooky things are hiding among the tiles 👻🤡👽 — clear every safe tile without digging one up to keep your cat safe!

Each opened tile shows how many spooky things touch it (its 8 neighbours). A blank tile has none, so its neighbours auto-clear.

Use those numbers to deduce where the spooky things are — every board is solvable by pure logic, no guessing. Switch to 🚩 mode (or right-click) to flag them.

Clear all safe tiles to win. Dig up a spooky thing and the cats bolt — 🙀 — but you can try the same board again.

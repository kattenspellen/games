A cozy place for every cat

Place exactly one cat for every basket. Each cat and its basket must share an edge. Every cat belongs to one basket; a cat cannot be shared. A cat may sit beside other baskets, as long as everyone can be paired one-to-one.

Two cats may never touch, even diagonally. Row and column numbers count cats, not baskets.

Tap a square to cycle cat → × → clear. Choose × Empty or right-click to leave an empty-space note. Tap a number to mark the rest of that line. Notes are optional.

Use arrow keys to move, Enter or Space to mark, and Ctrl/⌘ Z to undo. Orange squares hold cats; lavender squares hold the fixed baskets.

How to play

Place one cat 🐱 in every row, every column, and every color group.

Cats are shy — no two cats may touch, not even diagonally.

Tap a cell to place an ✕ note, tap again for a cat, again to clear.

Tap the ✕ buttons along the edges to X-out every empty tile in a whole row or column at once (tap again to clear those notes).

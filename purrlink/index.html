How to play

Every cat 🐱 has a matching ball of yarn 🧶 — same colour and number.

Drag a strand from each cat to its yarn (or yarn to cat). Strands can't cross or branch, and every square must be covered.

Drag back to shorten a strand; tap a cat or yarn to clear its strand. Drawing through another strand cuts it.

There's always exactly one answer, and you can reach it by logic alone.
